How it works

You get 5 starter packs when you pick a leaderboard name, plus 1 free pack every day. Sell your duplicate cards, and buy specific packs to chase Master Sets, in the Duplicates & Shop tab.

Click a card to flip it, or reveal them all at once. Click a flipped card to see it up close.

Achievements are earned by a single pack's cards, so every pack can earn them again.
